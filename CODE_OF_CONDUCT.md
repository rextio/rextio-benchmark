# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Rextio benchmark project a
harassment-free experience for everyone. We will be respectful, constructive,
and honest about both people and benchmark evidence.

## Expected behavior

- Treat differing experiences and viewpoints with respect.
- Give and accept specific, constructive feedback.
- Correct mistakes openly, especially mistakes in published evidence.
- Do not harass, insult, threaten, or disclose another person's private
  information.
- Do not manipulate, suppress, or selectively present benchmark results.

Maintainers may remove contributions or participation that violate these
standards.

## Scope and reporting

This policy applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project
spaces. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact path in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers will respect the reporter's privacy.

This policy is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
